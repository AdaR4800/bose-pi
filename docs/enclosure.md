# Enclosure Design

## 1. Purpose

The Bose-Pi project aims to integrate the Raspberry Pi 5, the digital audio interface, and the custom SmartSpeaker Bus interface into a compact physical assembly.

The enclosure should protect the electronics while keeping the required connections accessible.

## 2. Design Goals

The enclosure design should address:

- Compact dimensions and a low overall profile.
- Secure mounting of the Raspberry Pi and additional hardware.
- Access to the required power, network, and audio connections.
- Access to the SmartSpeaker Bus interface.
- Adequate ventilation for the installed components.
- Practical assembly and maintenance.
- Compatibility with 3D printing.

## 3. Hardware Integration

The planned assembly includes:

- Raspberry Pi 5 Model B.
- INNO-MAKER Digi One HAT for S/PDIF audio output.
- Custom SmartSpeaker Bus interface circuitry.
- External connectors required by the final circuit design.

The original RCA connector on the Digi One was removed to reduce the height and bulk of the assembly.

The final component positions and mounting arrangements remain subject to verification against the physical hardware.

## 4. Mechanical Documentation

The following information should be added when the design is finalized:

- Overall enclosure dimensions.
- Internal clearances and mounting points.
- Board and connector positions.
- Cable routing and strain relief.
- Ventilation openings.
- 3D-printing material and print settings.
- Photographs of the assembled hardware.
- Links to the source CAD files and exported STL files.

## 5. Current Status

The enclosure is a work in progress.

Exact dimensions, mounting details, and final connector positions are not specified in this document because they must be established from the actual assembly and CAD design.
